How to Install in 60 Seconds

Two simple sideload methods for your TV or development setup.

Method 1: Downloader App (Direct on TV)
  1. Install the free Downloader app from the Amazon Appstore or Google Play Store.
  2. Open Downloader, enter the direct release link for your device, and click Go.
  3. Click Install and grant permissions when prompted.
Method 2: Wireless ADB (PC / Mac)
# 1. Connect to your TV
adb connect 192.168.0.XXX:5555
# 2. Sideload the APK
adb install -r OnFire-v1.0-FireTV-Stick-Lite-4K-FireOS7.apk
# 3. Launch On Fire
adb shell am start -n com.onfire.launcher/.MainActivity
